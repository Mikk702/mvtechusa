Straight answers

The questions owners actually ask before they call.

What does MVTech do?
MVTech handles the paper side of an owner-operated business. We file every document — permits, policies, contracts, registrations — track every dollar in and out down to the line item, produce the quotes, invoices and forms your business sends out, and deliver a plain-English report on the first of each month. All of it is remote, and available nationwide.

Who is MVTech for?
Owner-operators who wear every hat — trades and contractors, food trucks and small restaurants, used car lots, solo attorneys and other professional practices, and people selling property For Sale By Owner. It fits best under roughly six employees, where the records live in a pile, a truck, a drawer, or the owner's head.

How much does MVTech cost?
There's a one-time $495 onboarding that builds your system up front, then a flat monthly price: Organized is $195 a month, Promoted is $395 a month, and Team Member is $695 a month. No hourly billing and no per-document charges — the monthly price is the price.

Do I have to learn new software?
No. There's no app to install and no system to log into and learn. Your spreadsheets open directly in Excel or Google Sheets, and your documents live in a normal folder. You keep working exactly the way you already work, and the organized picture comes back to you.

How is MVTech different from a bookkeeper or from accounting software?
A bank feed tells you that you spent $214 at a supply store. MVTech tells you what was in the cart, itemized line by line, because we read the actual receipt — detail bank feeds and store apps quietly throw away. We also handle documents, renewals and the paperwork you send out, which accounting software doesn't. MVTech is not a CPA firm and doesn't file taxes; we hand your preparer clean, finished numbers.

What do I actually receive each month?
A dashboard showing the whole business at a glance, searchable and updated daily; every dollar in and out itemized line by line; every document filed where you can find it; renewal and expiration reminders before dates come due; and a plain-English operations report available on the first, ready to hand to a tax preparer, a lender, or a buyer.

Do I own my records, and what happens if I leave?
You own everything. Every document is organized in a folder that's yours, in ordinary formats — spreadsheets and PDFs, not a proprietary system. There's no contract lock-in: leave whenever you want and take all of it with you.

Does MVTech work with businesses in my state?
Yes — MVTech works with owner-operated businesses anywhere in the United States. Every part of the service is remote: the onboarding call is a screen share, documents move by email and a folder you own, and your report arrives the same way. Nothing about it requires being in the same city or state as you.

How do I get started with MVTech?
Call or text 213-444-0024 and ask for Michael. The first conversation costs nothing: he'll show you exactly what he'd set up for your business and tell you straight if it isn't a fit. If you want to move ahead, one onboarding call of about an hour puts the whole system in place.
